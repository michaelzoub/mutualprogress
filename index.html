Mutual Progress

Understanding and accelerating technological progress.

We study the forces behind transformative technologies—and work to ensure the capabilities they create become broadly useful human agency.

Explore our goals ↓ Meet the team ↗

Five technologies. One continuing question: who gains the power to act?

01 — Goals

Progress should expand what people can do.

We connect technical possibility to the institutions, incentives, and people that determine whether progress becomes meaningful.

  1. 01

    Understand the forces of change.

    Study how frontier technologies emerge, compound, and move through society.

  2. 02

    Advance beneficial technology.

    Build and support systems with the potential to materially improve human life.

  3. 03

    Make capability mutual.

    Turn invention into broadly useful agency—not progress for a narrow few.

02 — Team
