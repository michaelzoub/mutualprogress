01 / Goals

Progress should expand what people can do.

We pursue technologies and ideas that create durable capability—not novelty for its own sake. Our work connects technical change to the institutions and people it affects.

  1. G.01

    Advance beneficial technology.

    Identify and accelerate technologies with the potential to meaningfully improve human life.

  2. G.02

    Understand transformation.

    Study how frontier systems interact with history, institutions, incentives, and human behavior.

  3. G.03

    Make capability mutual.

    Build tools and systems that turn invention into broadly useful, collective agency.
