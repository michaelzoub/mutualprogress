# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People evaluating ideas at the intersection of technology, institutions, history, and human agency. The landing page is their first orientation to Mutual Progress and should help them understand the initiative's purpose, research thesis, principles, and broader human context.

## Product Purpose

Mutual Progress is an independent research initiative exploring the ideas and technologies that expand human possibility. The landing page should make that purpose immediately clear and then guide visitors through the research agenda and its human stakes.

## Positioning

Mutual Progress treats technology as a human practice: technical ambition belongs in conversation with history, philosophy, economics, and the human sciences, and progress matters when new capability becomes broadly meaningful agency.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- This is a responsive, single-page landing experience built with Vite, semantic HTML, CSS, and GSAP.
- Navigation anchors visitors to Goals, Team, and Work with us.
- Motion is part of the product presentation and must remain purposeful, performant, keyboard-safe, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
- Claims must stay grounded in the supplied copy and assets; the page has no testimonials, customer logos, benchmarks, or commercial proof to fabricate.

## Brand Commitments

- The name is Mutual Progress.
- The central statement is “Understanding and accelerating technological progress.”
- The voice is precise, serious, optimistic, humanistic, and research-led.
- The supplied anthropological evolution reference is the primary visual direction.
- Warm research paper, disciplined grotesk typography, sparse institutional layouts, and a systematic green-to-cobalt technology sequence are durable identity traits.
- The human-to-technology evolution graphic is evidence of the long arc of capability, not editorial decoration.

## Product Principles

1. Make the core idea understandable before asking for deeper attention.
2. Connect frontier technology to the longer human story.
3. Treat progress as participatory agency, not capability alone.
4. Prefer durable questions and inspectable reasoning over predetermined answers.
5. Use restraint and precision to earn trust.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The page should preserve semantic structure, visible keyboard focus, sufficient contrast, touch-sized interactions, descriptive image alternatives, and a reduced-motion experience that retains comprehension without spatial movement.
